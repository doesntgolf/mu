←↓↑→ or hjkl: previous sibling, first child, parent, next sibling ·
    Enter or i: edit · I: type before, wrapping it · A: type after ·
    d: replace with a hole, or delete a row ·
    K/J: move a row up/down · o/O: insert a row after/before · u: undo

    and typing a token that's already next steps over it · Backspace at the start
    takes apart what's before · Enter: next hole, or done · Tab/Shift+Tab, or
    ←/→ at the ends: next/previous hole · Esc: done
